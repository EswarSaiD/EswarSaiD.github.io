Build Bridges - Not Fences!

In the world of Lust and Greed,
Fences grow like weeds,
What once was peaceful land,
Now transforms into a Battlefield.
Build bridges – Not Fences!

In this world of Lust and Greed,
Division is not what we need,
Unity is what we plead,
Build Bridges – Not Fences!

In this destructive World,
Will a White flag hoist high?
Will a happy truce be made?
Will borders erase, and the planet unify?

In this world of lust and greed,
Fences grow like weeds,
Build Bridges – Not Fences!
This is the way to succeed!

Mistakes Do Happen

mistakes do happen

a push or a fall,
may help us enlighten
although seems very small,

an apple's way down,
curious, Newton found,
where others just passed,
he saw a force, pulling fast

long ago in the past,
when the world was deemed flat,
this was called defective,
by a man with another perspective,
christened Aristotle,
the man was very thoughtful,

throughout humanity,
mistakes do happen
a push or a fall,
may help us enlighten
although seems very small.

Change

From the fire to the wheel,
All the way to steel,
From raw food and meat,
to a plateful of meal,
All of this is,
Change, I feel.

From algae to a tree,
From a drop to the sea,
From leaves to the tea,
Beans to coffee,
Wherever I see,
Change is what I perceive,

From the universe to a bee,
Wherever you see,
Change is what you feel,
You can't disagree,
This is my guarantee!
